## ☕ Brew Heaven Power BI Dashboard

### 📖 Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard for **Brew Heaven**, a local coffee shop chain, bringing together sales, inventory and customer feedback data.
The dashboard gives insights into:
- Sales trends and product performance
- Customer satisfaction and feedback
- Stock levels and reorder management

### 🧰 Features
- Two interactive report pages: **Sales Page** and **Management Page**
- Sales visualized **by category, time and product**
- **Customer feedback** integrated, with **average ratings** calculated
- **Power Query** used for data transformation and cleaning

### 💡 Skills Demonstrated
Power BI · Data Cleaning & Modeling · Data Visualization · DAX Calculations · Business Insights & Reporting

## 🛒 Ration Shop Stock & Sales Tracker (Excel)

### 📖 Overview
A stock and sales tracker for a small ration (grocery) shop, built in **Microsoft Excel**. Sales and purchases go in; Excel works out prices, current stock and what to reorder, and shows it on one dashboard.
*All data is made up, for demonstration only.*

### 💡 Skills Demonstrated
Excel Tables · XLOOKUP · SUMIFS · Data Validation · IF · COUNTIF · Conditional Formatting · Pivot Tables · Pivot Charts

## 📈 Sales Dashboard: Regions, Products & Targets (Excel)

### 📖 Overview
A sales dashboard for a snacks and beverages distributor, built in **Microsoft Excel**: sales by region, category and customer, month-to-month growth, and target vs actual for each salesperson. One slicer filters every pivot.
*All data is made up, for demonstration only.*

Excel Tables · XLOOKUP · SUMIFS · COUNTIFS · TEXT & DATE · Conditional Formatting · Pivot Tables · Slicers · Charts
